This policy explains the information that may be processed through our website, including details you choose to submit through our contact form. Please review it carefully. The exact information processed can depend on how you use the website and which third-party services are active.

NKTech Studio provides digital services such as app development, website development, digital marketing, and custom digital solutions.

For privacy questions or requests relating to information submitted through this website, contact us using the email address listed at the end of this policy.

02 — Information We May Collect

When you use the contact form, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and the message or project details you choose to share.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through the contact form.

Information you submit may be used to respond to enquiries, understand your project requirements, communicate with you, provide requested information, and maintain the security and operation of the website.

We do not state that information is used for any purpose beyond those described here unless you are informed separately or the use is otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

The website uses EmailJS to transmit contact-form submissions. Information entered into the form is sent to that service to help deliver your message. Its processing is also subject to the third party’s own terms and privacy policy.

This website does not intentionally ask you to enter personal information through cookies. However, hosting providers, embedded content, or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies depending on their configuration. You can review and manage cookie settings in your browser.

If analytics, advertising, or additional tracking tools are added to this website in the future, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. No method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Contact enquiries may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage business communications, maintain records, or meet legal obligations. Retention by third-party service providers is also subject to their policies and settings.

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request a correction, or ask for deletion where applicable law permits. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

You can choose not to submit information through the contact form, though this may limit our ability to respond to your enquiry. You can also manage browser cookies through your browser settings.

08 — Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for people seeking information about digital services and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.
